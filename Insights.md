# CPI Inflation Analysis — Insights

## 1. Category Contribution
- Food accounts for about **44.4%** of classified observations in the workbook.
- Luxury contributes about **14.8%**, and Clothing about **11.1%**; the other individual categories contribute 7.4% or less.
- Because Food has the largest share of observations, food-price changes deserve close attention in the overall CPI review. This share is a dataset composition measure, not the official CPI expenditure weight.

## 2. Year-over-Year CPI Inflation
- The workbook reports the highest overall annual inflation around **2022 at approximately 6.6%**, before easing to about **3.2% in 2023**.
- Inflation pressure increased during 2020–2022.
- Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban series broadly follow similar movements, suggesting a shared overall trend across these sectors.

## 3. Food Price Analysis
- Vegetables are highlighted as one of the more volatile food categories.
- Fruits also show fluctuations, including a rise in February 2023 followed by a decline in May.
- Milk and pulses show more consistently positive movement across many observed months.
- These are patterns in the workbook's CPI index series; they do not, by themselves, explain the causes of price changes.

## 4. Pre- and Post-COVID Comparison
- The workbook compares Food & Beverages, Health, and Household Goods & Services across annual and monthly views.
- Food & Beverages show pronounced movement through the COVID-era period, with a strong rise in late 2020 and easing in early 2021.
- Compare category trends separately because the categories can respond differently over time.

## 5. Fuel & Light Correlations
The workbook reports these correlations with Fuel & Light:

| CPI component | Correlation | Interpretation |

| Transport & Communication | 0.66 | Stronger positive association among the pairs analysed |
| Clothing & Footwear | 0.42 | Moderate positive association |
| Health | 0.40 | Moderate positive association |
| Household Goods & Services | 0.31 | Weak positive association |
| Education | 0.23 | Weak positive association |

- The strongest positive relationship in these comparisons is with Transport & Communication.
- The Food & Beverages pair has a weak negative correlation in the selected series.
- Correlation does not establish causation. Results depend on the period, index series, and calculation method used.
